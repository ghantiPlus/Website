2. Information We Collect

a. Phone Number

We collect your mobile phone number for OTP-based authentication. This is how we verify your identity when you create an account or sign in. One phone number can hold more than one kind of account (for example a resident account and a peddler account), each signed in separately.

b. Peddler Location

If you use the peddler app, we collect your precise GPS location while you are live. Location is collected from the moment you go live until you go offline, including while the app is in the background or the screen is off. While it is running, the app shows a notification so you can see it. Each position sample includes latitude, longitude, accuracy, speed, heading and time. We use it to work out where you are, so residents of a locality can see that you are nearby. We do not collect your location when you are not live.

c. Resident Locality and Addresses

The resident app does not read your device location. You choose your locality yourself, and you can save up to ten addresses, each with a label you choose, a locality and up to two nearby landmarks. We use them to show you the peddlers and events in your area.

d. Profile Information

  • Residents: the display name you provide.
  • Peddlers: display name, a short description, city, the name of a stand if you hold one, and a referral code if you enter one.
  • Event organisers: display name, and the details of each event you post (title, venue, dates).

A peddler's account may be marked as verified after a person from our local team checks them in person. We record only the decision, who made it and when it expires. We do not collect or store identity documents for this.

e. Photos

Peddlers can add photos of what they have brought that day, and event organisers can add a poster image. You take the photo with your camera or pick it from your gallery through your phone's own picker. Photos are checked automatically and may be reviewed by our local team before residents see them.

f. Voice Recordings

Peddlers and event organisers can record a short voice message for residents (up to 30 seconds for an event). The recording is uploaded to our servers and played to residents of the area it is for. When you delete a recording it stops being delivered to residents.

g. Messages to Peddlers

Residents can send short messages (up to 280 characters) about a peddler's post for the day. Messages appear in a thread shared by the residents of the same housing society. Messages do not show who wrote them; the peddler sees the text, not your name or phone number. You can withdraw a message, which removes its text.

h. Usage and Crash Data

The apps send us first-party usage events: screens viewed, actions taken, a session identifier, the app version and your operating system (for example Android), together with your account identifier and account type. If the app crashes, we receive the error message and technical stack trace. We do not collect your device model or advertising identifiers.

Our servers also keep technical logs (access times and errors), and we record that a photo or voice file was viewed, by which type of account and from which locality.

i. Communications

We collect any messages you send to us by email, WhatsApp or other means.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Phone Number: To authenticate you by OTP, deliver account-related messages, and respond to you.
  • Peddler Location: To show residents which peddlers are near them, and to run the day's live session.
  • Resident Locality and Addresses: To show relevant peddlers and events for your area.
  • Profile Information: To identify you in the apps and to show peddlers and events to residents.
  • Photos and Voice Recordings: To show peddlers' and organisers' announcements to residents, and to keep content within our rules.
  • Messages: To let residents ask a peddler about the day's post.
  • Usage and Crash Data: To fix errors, understand how the apps are used and improve them.
  • Communications: To answer your questions and resolve support requests.

We share information with the following service providers, strictly for the purposes below:

a. SMS Gateway

We use {{ site.data.legal.third_parties.sms_gateway }} to deliver OTP messages to your phone number. Your phone number is shared with this provider for SMS delivery, and it tells us whether a message was delivered.

b. Cloud Infrastructure

Our servers and the photo and voice files you upload are hosted with {{ site.data.legal.third_parties.cloud_provider }}.

c. Map Tiles

The peddler and event-organiser apps show a map using tiles from {{ site.data.legal.third_parties.map_provider }}, which uses data from OpenStreetMap contributors. When a map is shown, your device requests tiles directly from that service, so it receives your device's IP address and the map area you are viewing. We do not send it your account details.

We require service providers to process your data only as instructed by us and in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

We do not use any third-party analytics or advertising SDK. Usage analytics are collected first-party, on our own infrastructure, and are never sold or shared with advertisers. We do not integrate a payment processor, because the apps are free to use.

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • Encryption in transit: Data between the apps and our servers is protected with TLS.
  • Access Controls: Access to personal data is restricted to authorised personnel on a need-to-know basis, with role-based access controls in place.
  • Audit Trail: We maintain logs of data access and modifications to detect and respond to unauthorised activity.
  • Security Log Retention: Security logs are retained for a minimum of {{ site.data.legal.data_retention.security_logs_days }} days (1 year) in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025.

While we take reasonable steps to protect your data, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

7. Your Rights Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

Consent

By using the apps, you consent to the collection and processing of your personal data as described in this Privacy Policy. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us or by requesting deletion of your account, subject to the provisions of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

Your Rights as a Data Principal

Under the DPDP Act, 2023, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: You have the right to obtain a summary of your personal data being processed and the processing activities undertaken with that data.
  • Right to Correction: You have the right to request correction of inaccurate or misleading personal data, and completion of incomplete personal data. Profile details can also be edited in the app.
  • Right to Erasure: You have the right to request erasure of your personal data that is no longer necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You have the right to withdraw your consent for data processing at any time by contacting us.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: You have the right to register a grievance with our Grievance Officer regarding any data processing concerns.
  • Right to Nominate: You have the right to nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.

Response Timeline

We will respond to your requests within 90 days of receipt, as stipulated under the DPDP Act, 2023.

Data Protection Board

If you are not satisfied with our response to your grievance, you have the right to file a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India established under the DPDP Act, 2023.

10. Device Permissions

The apps request only the permissions below.

  • Location (precise and approximate), peddler app only: To share your position while you are live. It keeps running with the screen off, and stops when you go offline. The resident and event-organiser apps do not ask for location.
  • Foreground service (location), peddler app only: Lets the live session continue in the background while a notification is shown.
  • Notifications, peddler app only: To show that notification. We do not send push notifications.
  • Microphone, peddler and event-organiser apps: To record a voice message. The microphone is used only while you are recording, never in the background.
  • Camera and photos: The apps use your phone's own camera and gallery picker, so they see only the photo you choose.
  • Internet: To sync data and communicate with our servers.
  • Vibration: To provide haptic feedback.
