How to Delete Your Account

{{ site.data.site.app_name }} respects your right to delete your account and associated data. You can request permanent deletion of your account using either of the methods described below.

Option 1: From the App

In the resident or peddler app, open {{ site.data.legal.account_deletion.in_app_path | join: " > " }}. This brings you to this page, where you send the request by email as described below.

Option 2: Request Deletion by Email

Send an email to {{ site.data.legal.account_deletion.email }} with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

Please include the following in your email:

  • Your registered phone number
  • Which account to delete: resident, peddler, or event organiser (one number can have more than one)
  • Reason for deletion (optional)

What Gets Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data will be permanently removed:

  • Profile information
  • Phone number
  • Saved addresses and your chosen locality
  • Photos, posters and voice recordings you uploaded
  • Any peddler location samples still held (these are deleted automatically after {{ site.data.legal.data_retention.location_days }} days in any case)

What May Be Retained

Certain data may be retained to comply with legal obligations:

  • Security logs (retained for {{ site.data.legal.data_retention.security_logs_days }} days per DPDP Rules 2025)
  • The record of a peddler's in-person verification, kept as an audit record
  • Usage and crash events, until they reach the end of the retention period in our Privacy Policy
  • Anything we must keep to meet legal obligations or resolve disputes, for up to {{ site.data.legal.data_retention.account_data_after_deletion_days }} days

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are processed within {{ site.data.legal.account_deletion.processing_time }}.

Note: Once deletion is processed, it cannot be reversed.

Contact Us

For questions about account deletion, contact {{ site.data.site.contact_email }}.
